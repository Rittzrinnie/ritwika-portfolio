PROJECT OVERVIEW

Volkswagen R is the high-performance sub-brand of Volkswagen, premium, performance-driven, and aspirational.

The Virtual Studio was its first fully digital product experience: a move toward immersive, high-end digital engagement. The goal wasn't only to showcase performance vehicles, but to create a digital environment that felt exclusive, powerful, and technologically advanced. My role was to keep that premium experience intuitive, structured, and accessible, without diluting its performance-driven identity.

I proposed the AR integration concept and owned the end-to-end UX direction, including user flows, information architecture, interaction patterns, screen hierarchy and the overall onboarding experience.

THE PROBLEM

How might Volkswagen R recreate the thrill of seeing a performance car in person, for customers who research and buy online?

CONTEXT

Customers increasingly research cars online, yet static images and videos can't match the thrill of seeing a performance vehicle in person. Volkswagen R needed a solution that would:

  1. 01Evoke the excitement of R-brand performance across digital channels.
  2. 02Drive deeper online engagement and configurator interactions.
  3. 03Bridge showrooms and e-commerce through augmented reality.
  4. 04Lower reliance on physical demo cars.
THE OBJECTIVE

Redefine the digital car-shopping experience with an immersive, interactive platform, one that's both visually stunning and functionally intuitive, meeting modern buyers' expectations for interactivity, personalization, and mobile-friendly AR.

RESEARCH & DISCOVERY

With the senior designer, I mapped how users move from inspiration to evaluation when exploring high-performance vehicles online.

KEY FOCUS
  • →Understanding where users drop off.
  • →Identifying moments of emotional engagement.
  • →Defining transition points to configurator and dealer inquiry.
A "Model-First" architecture

I structured the platform so the 3D vehicle is the hero, keeping UI controls minimal and contextual.

DESIGN PRIORITIES
  • →Maximum visual real estate.
  • →Simple, discoverable customization controls.
  • →Clear call-to-action for the configurator.

PROTOTYPING & VALIDATION

High-fidelity prototypes tested real-time interaction, AR entry points, and customization usability. What we validated:

Ease of 360° navigation
Discoverability of features
Performance perception
Smooth transition to next steps
WHAT CHANGED

Testing showed that users needed a clearer entry point into the AR experience and more guidance on what to do next. I simplified the flow, strengthened the CTA hierarchy and reduced unnecessary steps so the experience felt more intuitive.

CONSTRAINTS & TRADE-OFFS
Immersion vs. performance

Balancing an immersive AR/3D experience with mobile performance and technical feasibility.

Brand consistency

Keeping the experience and design assets consistent with the VW R visual language and design system, while avoiding unnecessary complexity.

SOLUTION · VW R DIGITAL PORTFOLIO

Together with the VW R team, I helped create a digital portfolio of every R model, letting users navigate and explore each car and its capabilities digitally. It became one of the most complete digital products in the VW R line.

CONCLUSION

The VW R Virtual Studio turns the showroom into an interactive, immersive experience, exploring R models in 3D, customizing colors and features, and engaging with the cars in a premium, exploratory environment. It shows how immersion and customization can elevate digital experiences, deepen user connection, and strengthen brand perception in automotive.
