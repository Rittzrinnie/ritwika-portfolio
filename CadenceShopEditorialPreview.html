02 / At a glance

A simpler path through a complex buying journey.

The challenge was to support independent software buying without losing the clarity, reliability and accessibility required in a specialist B2B environment.

The problem

Product discovery, account access and purchase workflows needed more understandable next steps for customers.

Design focus

Research-informed journeys, accessible interfaces, reusable components and launch-ready workflows.

03 / Pivotal question

How do you remove friction without oversimplifying the product?

The most useful case-study material is the reasoning behind navigation, feedback states, account access and transactional steps—not just a collection of final screens.

04 / Design decisions

Make the next step obvious.

Your original Figma file contains account registration, login, recovery, responsive layouts and UX audit material. A presentation board specifically documents clearer input-error messages, contextual information icons, and success/error messaging.

View original Figma evidence ↗

Explain the issue

Use field-level messages so users can understand what needs correcting rather than deciphering generic feedback.

Guide recovery

Use contextual help and visible success states to make account-access journeys easier to follow.

05 / The system

Build patterns, not isolated pages.

Cadence Shop used Cuboids design-system components alongside accessibility reviews, responsive design, staging testing and design documentation.
